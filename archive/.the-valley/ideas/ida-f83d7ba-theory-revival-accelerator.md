# The knowledge graph is a theory-revival accelerator

Prior art: Naur, "Programming as Theory Building" (1985)
(https://gwern.net/doc/cs/algorithm/1985-naur.pdf). A program is a theory held by its builders, in
Ryle's sense: the capacity to explain why the thing is shaped as it is, what in it corresponds to
what in the world, and which modifications fit its nature. Text and documentation are artifacts of
the theory, never the theory itself. A program dies when its theory-holders disperse; revival from
text alone yields a different theory; and modification without the theory accretes locally
plausible, globally incoherent patches.

Agent-authored development is that death scenario run continuously: every run is a fresh mind that
reads, modifies, and disperses. The graph is the countermeasure, and Naur names its job precisely:
not documentation — a theory-revival accelerator. Its measure is revival cost: how quickly a fresh
mind rebuilds enough theory to modify without violating it.

The graph's conventions restate as theory hygiene. A node presents only current design because a
theory is a present understanding, not the history of its acquisition. Restatement over accretion,
because accreted addenda are theory-less modification in prose. Project knowledge is singular
([[ida-f172c8e]],
[ida-f172c8e-project-knowledge-not-agent-instructions.md](./ida-f172c8e-project-knowledge-not-agent-instructions.md))
because there is one theory, not a per-audience gloss. Neural nodes self-obsoleting into
deterministic ones ([[ida-b48bded]],
[ida-b48bded-production-dags-and-events.md](./ida-b48bded-production-dags-and-events.md)) is theory
being compiled into the one form that refuses violations without a person present. What no check yet
holds is exactly what human review is for: theory conformance.

Against Naur's own conclusion — that no artifact regime substitutes for living theory-holders — the
project makes a wager: when capture is cheap and immediate at the moment of decision, the unwritable
residue is far smaller than his era's practice suggested, and what remains is re-derivable by a
strong reader from the right written skeleton. The project is the experiment.
