# Keep architectural commitments concise and preserve their supporting ideas

The architecture document states the outcome-DAG and federation commitments at a high level. Their
detailed reasoning lives in knowledge-graph nodes until the relevant design work is needed.

[[ida-eac723e]](../ideas/ida-eac723e-outcome-dag.md) preserves the outcome-DAG proposal.
[[ida-8482624]](../ideas/ida-8482624-federation-groups.md) preserves the proposal for cooperation
between valleys. [self-transparency.md](../../design/self-transparency.md) carries the rationale for
making the system's own operation inspectable.

Detailed outcome and federation design documents are to be developed when their
[user scenarios](../../design/user-scenarios.md) become priorities: S3 and later for scheduling, S5
and later for federation. The idea nodes supply the source material for that work.

This decision records the disposition of the material introduced in PR #1 and placed at these
document levels through PR #5.
