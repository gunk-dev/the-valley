# Research reports

This directory holds research and critical analysis outside the project's knowledge graph. Reports
describe a dated repository snapshot, review external literature and systems, and propose questions
and experiments. They do not establish project policy or change the status of knowledge nodes.

- [The foundations and research opportunities of the-valley](the-valley-foundations/README.md),
  2026-09-20. A study of portable verification, integration, distributed history, trust, project
  knowledge, and outcome-driven agent work. Includes system comparisons, annotated primary sources,
  a reading curriculum, and a proposed experimental agenda.

[compatibility EPUB](the-valley-foundations/the-valley-research-revised.epub) and a
[PDF](the-valley-foundations/the-valley-research-scribe-revised.pdf), with
[delivery and rebuild instructions](the-valley-foundations/reader-edition/README.md).
