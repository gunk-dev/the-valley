usage: valley flow [<args>]

Runs valley-flow, the client that launches and follows agent runs, in place
of valley, with the arguments after flow and the environment valley started
in: the terminal, signals and exit status are valley-flow's. valley-flow is
found on PATH; the flake's klaus package installs it. flow needs no checkout,
and every argument is valley-flow's, so valley flow --help and
valley flow --version are its help and version. Without an executable
valley-flow on PATH, flow says so and exits 127.
