  pending [--all] [--repo <name>] [--checks] [--drafts] [--branches]
          [--json] [-v] [--known-signers <file>] [--integrator-name <name>]
                   list what waits on you and what is moving. one line
                   on main's state comes first for each project whose
                   policy declares follow checks: clear, clearing or
                   muddy. needs you holds the ready changes that wait on
                   an approval, one numbered row each in landing order:
                   oldest ready first, as the check worker builds them,
                   then by project and change id. a row gives the
                   handle and active version, its checks as passed out of
                   required (or how many are pending, or ✗ the pending
                   checks whose newest build by the host's check worker
                   failed, and when), and how long it has been ready,
                   with its title under it. a stack of
                   such changes is one row, its top's, saying `stack of
                   N`: approving the top lands every layer. moving holds
                   the ready changes that need no approval now, one line
                   each: ◐ approved, waiting on checks and the
                   integrator; ✗ a check failed, named; ✗ or ◇ a restack
                   holds it. under a row, conflicts with <handle> names
                   the project's other ready changes it conflicts with:
                   both heads merged on main's tip, as the integrator
                   merges one once the other lands. layers of one stack
                   are never compared. a pair with a head not on main's
                   tip awaits a restack, and at most 64 merges run; one
                   line under the sections counts the pairs not
                   compared, and why. each answer is kept in the client
                   cache, so a warm listing runs no merge. older
                   versions are never listed. a last line
                   counts the drafts and the branches no change is on.
                   lines fit the terminal, or 80 columns off one; titles
                   are cut short with …, names never. names are bold and
                   glyphs coloured on a terminal unless NO_COLOR is set.
                     --all        every project the serving host
                                  publishes, as one listing: each line
                                  names its project, and the rows of
                                  every project share one landing order
                                  (see valley help changes)
                     --repo NAME  that one project
                     --checks     add to each row the checks the policy
                                  requires of it, by cost, as checks
                                  derives them, and its full check state,
                                  naming the checks that failed, the
                                  pending checks that failed in the
                                  worker's build, and what they cost the
                                  host's check worker to build, with a
                                  see: line that reads the worker's
                                  report of a failure. that report is
                                  diagnostic: a check's state comes from
                                  notes alone. with --drafts, give each
                                  draft's block the same lines, its name
                                  line saying its checks as a row does.
                                  name the conflicted paths of each
                                  change a row conflicts with.
                                  with --json, give each pending change,
                                  and with --drafts each draft, its
                                  checks object
                     --drafts     list the drafts in full, in place of
                                  their count
                     --branches   list the branches no change is on in
                                  full, in place of their count. each
                                  draft and branch is a block: its name
                                  with its head's age and author, its
                                  title, then the commits and the diff
                                  it adds to the nearest listed entry
                                  its head contains, or to main. a stack
                                  is its top, then every entry the top
                                  contains, at one indentation; an entry
                                  not on the one listed after it says
                                  `on <base>`
                     --json       print valley-pending/v1: the
                                  valley-changes/v1 shape, with the
                                  pending changes in changes, each
                                  marked approved or not, with its
                                  conflicts and conflicts_unknown, and
                                  the drafts in drafts
                     -v           follow each row with its block, its
                                  stack drawn in full, with what its
                                  head includes, `uncertain` when it may
                                  have landed without its evidence
                                  stored, its conflicts' paths as
                                  --checks names them, and its full
                                  check state; and
                                  warn on stderr when a plan graph is
                                  missing or unreadable
                     --known-signers FILE, --integrator-name NAME
                                  what a transfer statement is verified
                                  with (see changes below)
                   for each project with a ready change, or a draft
                   --drafts --checks lists, reading the checks reads the
                   policy once and runs attest verify for each note.
