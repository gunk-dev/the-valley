usage: valley ready [--head <sha>] [--change <id>] [--if-active <sha>] [--stamp] [<branch>]

  ready [--head <sha>] [--change <id>] [--if-active <sha>] [--stamp] [<branch>]
                   mark a head of the branch ready, as a new version of its
                   change, in the change's record on origin:
                   refs/the-valley/changes/main/<change id>. the branch
                   defaults to the current one. the head is --head, or else
                   origin/<branch>, and it must be on origin already. the
                   first ready of a change makes v1; each later one adds
                   the next version, at a head other than the active
                   version's. a draft or a withdrawn change comes back this
                   way: at the active version's head, it is ready again at
                   that version, and at another head it gains the next
                   version. prints the handle and version, e.g.
                   `app nxvp v2 ready`.
                   the change id is --change, else the head commit's jj
                   change-id header, else a Change-Id: trailer in its
                   message, else a new one, which is printed. a header and
                   a trailer that disagree are refused. so is a change
                   whose commits from its merge base with origin/main hold
                   a merge, or that has more than one merge base.
                     --if-active SHA
                                  add the version only while the change
                                  is ready at an active version whose head
                                  is SHA. the record ref is leased on the
                                  record read, so nothing comes between
                                  that check and the write. rebase readies
                                  the head it rebased this way.
                     --stamp      amend the head of your local branch to
                                  carry the id as a Change-Id: trailer and
                                  push the branch, leased, before marking it
                                  ready. it only starts a new change: it
                                  is refused for a change that has a
                                  record on origin already, and for a head
                                  already on origin.
                   the record names the principal your key pushes as, read
                   from git config valley.principal; the host refuses one
                   naming anyone else. the record ref is pushed leased on
                   the value read. a new change is owned by the principal
                   that readies it, and a later version may come from its
                   owner, the operator, or a restack holder while the
                   change is ready.
                   a record never makes anything land, and approve is
                   still the only way to main. the integrator reads
                   records only to refuse: it lands no request that would
                   bring a version of a change that is not ready.
